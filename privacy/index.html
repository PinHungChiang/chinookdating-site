Privacy Policy — Chinook

Operator: Pin-Hung Chiang, Calgary, Alberta, Canada
Contact: support@chinookdating.com
Effective date: October 28, 2026

What we collect

  • Waitlist data: if you join the waitlist on this website, we store your email address and use it only to notify you about the launch. You can ask us to remove it at any time via the contact below.
  • Account data: email address, password (stored hashed by our auth provider), date of birth (used to verify you are 18+; we calculate age and do not publish your birthday).
  • Profile data you provide: display name, introduction text, city, interests and preferences, and up to six photos you upload.
  • Optional verification data: if you choose selfie review, the selfie evidence you submit for that review. It is private, never shown on your public profile, and submitting it does not by itself verify identity.
  • Location (optional, coarse only): if you explicitly enable nearby discovery and consent again in the app, we store a coarse grid cell (about 0.05°) derived from your device location — never your precise GPS coordinates — for up to six hours unless you stop sharing sooner.
  • Communications: messages you send to your matches, invitations and activity plans you create or respond to, and your confirmations.
  • Safety data: reports you file (the person reported, category, and your description), moderation review records created by our safety team, and block/unmatch actions.
  • Technical data: our infrastructure providers automatically keep basic technical logs (such as IP address, device and app version, and error logs) needed to operate and secure the service. We do not currently run third-party advertising or behavioural analytics.

How we use it

To operate the service (accounts, matching, messaging, invitations, activities, nearby discovery), to enforce consent-based visibility (your private profile is never public unless you review and publish it), for safety and abuse prevention (report review, account enforcement), and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Who can see what

  • Your private profile (photos, birthday, email) is visible only to you, except safety/moderation staff under access controls when needed to review a report.
  • Your public profile shows only what you reviewed and published: name, age, introduction, city, interests, preferences.
  • Exact venues in private plans are shown only to the participants who confirmed them; public activity cards never include exact venues.
  • Service providers that host the service process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, storage), Resend (email delivery), and GitHub Pages (hosting of this website).

Retention and deletion

  • Coarse nearby location expires automatically (six hours) or when you stop sharing.
  • You may request account deletion in the app. Deletion freezes your account immediately; private data is then erased under our retention schedule, except safety reports and moderation records, which may be retained while needed for safety and legal reasons and are then purged.
  • Backups are retained for a limited period and then overwritten.

Your choices

You can edit or unpublish your public profile, stop location sharing, block or unmatch any person, withdraw consent-based features at any time, and request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting support@chinookdating.com. We respond within the timelines required by applicable law.

Adults only

Chinook is for people 18 or older. Accounts found to belong to minors are removed and their data deleted.

Changes

We will announce material changes in the app before they take effect.
